Where Regulatory Control Stops

Instrumentation Isn't the Same as Intelligence

A powerhouse needs instrumentation to measure, control, and protect its equipment and people. Regulatory control alone still leaves control and management gaps that EMRS is built to close.

  • Instruments cover voltage, current, frequency, power, temperature, pressure, flow, vibration, speed, fuel, steam, water, lubrication, and cooling
  • Control gap: interacting pressure controllers create instability
  • Control gap: turbines and other equipment sit outside the integrated system
  • Control gap: steam generators are blind to lower-header conditions
  • Management gap: lowest cost isn't always the operator's actual rule set
  • Management gap: no intelligence layer adjusts for changing equipment, fuel, or business conditions
The Controller

MultiMaxx: A Rule-Based Fuzzy Logic Controller

A new class of controller that supports multiple advanced process control optimizers, runs in milliseconds, and honors the plant's own operating modes.

  • Integrates all utility equipment into one intelligent system
  • Measures equipment and fuel performance in real time for predictive header control
  • Reports not just what went wrong, but how to do better
  • Runs manual, automatic, or advanced process control without breaking overall system behavior
Beyond the Powerhouse

UltraMaxx: Business Units, Powerhouse, and Utilities

UltraMaxx adds anticipatory control: the powerhouse tracks business-unit demand and can participate directly in demand response, real-time pricing, and virtual power plant programs.

  • Rapidly adjusts the powerhouse to business-unit demand
  • Manages electric and fossil fuel together
  • Supports demand response, real-time pricing, and virtual power plant programs
  • Integrates third-party demand and weather forecasting
System Architecture

EMRS Structure: Allocation, Advisory, and Reporting

Allocation System, Process Advisor, Energy Advocate, and Support Services & Reporting sit above the powerhouse, utilities, and business units, working as one coordinated layer rather than separate point solutions.

Light & Medium Industrial

ControlMaxx: A DCS in a Box

Smaller facilities get the same closed-loop benefits as large industrial sites, delivered through ControlMaxx: a high-performance DCS in a box with Process Advisor, Energy Advocate, predefined layouts, and AI configuration tools built in.

Internal processing runs in milliseconds on cost-effective standard servers. BridgeMaxx connects to your existing DCS, PLC, turbine-generator, and gas-turbine systems, so ControlMaxx layers on top of what you already have rather than replacing it.

The Path Forward

From Assessment to Sustained Performance

An AI-enhanced workflow: assessment identifies opportunity where savings alone don't tell the whole story, execution uses EMRS analytics to stand the system up, and ongoing maintenance and service works with the operator to shape long-term behavior.

Assessment & Execution

  • Level 0 identifies opportunity from one to two years of energy spend and process data
  • Levels 1 and 2 qualify and quantify the opportunity with a digital twin
  • Level 3 adjusts constraints to uncover further opportunity
  • Design development uses the reporting system to guide corrections, through installation and test

Commissioning, Maintenance & Service

  • System commissioning confirms the design against real operating conditions
  • Performance testing drives behavior adjustments before sign-off
  • Operations refresher training keeps the team current as the system evolves
  • Ongoing maintenance, service, and reporting sustain performance long-term
