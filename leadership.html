Ron L. Childress
Director of R&D

Ron is the primary research investigator at Dynamic Energy Systems, the architect of the patented control technology that defines DES's competitive position. His career has been devoted to the development of advanced software and control system platforms across the heavy industrial and utility sectors, with the last twelve years focused specifically on Real-Time Pricing control technologies that solve complex, multi-variable optimization problems at industrial scale.

To address the full scope of large-scale energy management challenges, Ron developed a rule-based controller for which he holds an applications patent and has since expanded the system's capabilities with additional patents pending. The result is the EMRS platform: a new class of Energy Management and Reporting System that solves the most difficult process control problems the industry presents, and has been validated through documented client results at facilities across North America.

Ron is the author of multiple technical papers and journal articles, including the foundational DES research published in the Association of Energy Engineers Journal that established the theoretical and practical framework for autonomous closed-loop industrial energy management. He is also the inventor and primary author of the MultiMaxx operational theory, the technical foundation for DES's flagship boiler control product.

James E. Robinson
PE · P.Eng · CEM · CEP

James Robinson brings more than thirty years of experience in power and control system projects across the industrial and utility sectors, a career that spans R&D support in mining equipment development, field liaison engineering, senior design engineering at Catalytic Construction, and more than a decade at Honeywell where he worked with emerging Distributed Control System and PLC technologies across marketing, turnkey project delivery, and product development functions.

During that period he published multiple papers in the energy field that contributed to the professional knowledge base the industry relies on today.

Jim joined DES to implement EMRS technology for the reduction of heavy industrial energy usage, bringing to the team the combination of engineering rigor, field project experience, and audit methodology precision that underpins DES's ability to quantify client opportunities with the specificity necessary to fund and justify major capital commitments.
