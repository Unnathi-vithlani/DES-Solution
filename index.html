The Problem We Solve

Why Industrial Energy Systems Need Cruise Control

Industrial facilities face increasing pressure: volatile energy prices, strict emissions regulations, aging equipment, and the constant demand for higher reliability. Recovery boilers, powerhouses, and combustion systems are incredibly complex - a single upset or inefficient operation can mean significant lost production, higher costs, and increased carbon emissions.

⚠

Operators Fighting Fires, Not Optimizing

When operators spend their shifts reacting to process upsets, facility performance suffers. Traditional control systems frequently fall short when it comes to predictive, multi-unit, and economic optimization - leaving real money on the table every hour.

⚠

Complex Multi-Fuel Operations

Controlling multiple fuels across multiple boilers while observing hundreds of constraints every second is beyond any human operator's capacity. The result is a persistent gap between what your facility produces and what it could produce.

⚠

Missed Economic Windows

Real-time energy price signals create and close economic opportunities in minutes. By the time an operator recognizes a signal, calculates the optimal response, and executes the change, the window has often closed - and the savings vanish.

At DES Global, we change that.
