The Cost of Variability

You Can't Manage What You Don't Measure. We Quantify It First.

Before any control intervention, DES engineering audits quantify the specific cost of variability at your facility - in financial terms your organization can act on. Most clients are surprised by the magnitude. The gap between average performance and consistent near-optimum operation is typically measured in millions of dollars annually at energy-intensive facilities.

01

Process Variability Audit & Quantification

DES engineers analyze your historical process data to identify the specific sources of variability - control loop performance, equipment limitations, operator technique variability, and process disturbances and quantify the financial impact of each. This analysis forms the foundation of the business case for variability management investment.

02

Advanced Process Control Deployment

DES variability management solutions deploy advanced process controls specifically calibrated to your facility's process dynamics - reducing the variance in product quality, steam pressure, fuel consumption, and production rates that conventional PID loops cannot adequately address.

03

Conservative Target Elimination

When operators cannot trust the consistency of their control system, they set conservative targets - leaving real economic capacity unused as a safety margin against variability-induced excursions. As variability is eliminated, conservative targets can be systematically moved toward true economic limits, releasing capacity that was always physically present but never safely accessible.

04

Equipment Longevity Improvement

Variability accelerates equipment wear through thermal cycling, pressure excursions, and mechanical stress that consistent control eliminates. Reduced variability translates directly to extended equipment life, reduced maintenance frequency, and fewer unplanned shutdowns - benefits that compound financially over the life of the installation.
